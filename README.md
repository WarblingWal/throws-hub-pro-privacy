# Privacy Policy for Throws Hub Pro

**Last updated:** 11 November 2026

Throws Hub Pro ("the App") is built as an offline-first athletic tracking tool. Your privacy is fully respected.

## Data Collection and Storage
- **No Personal Data Collected:** Throws Hub Pro does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, usage analytics, or crash telemetry with the developer or any third parties.
- **Local-Only Storage:** All athlete profiles, throw distances, pentathlon scorecards, and settings you enter are stored exclusively on your device's local storage.
- **No Internet Access Required:** The App operates completely offline and does not connect to external servers.

## Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer via the support email listed on the Google Play Store page.
